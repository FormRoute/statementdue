Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

What we collect

We collect only the information needed to prepare and submit your Statement of Information: entity name and number, business addresses, officer/director or member/manager names and addresses, agent information, business type, your email, and the signer's name and title. We do not collect Social Security numbers or financial account information. Payments are processed by Stripe — we never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

  • To prepare and submit your filing to the California Secretary of State.
  • To email you your filed copy, confirmations, and (if you opt in) deadline reminders.
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers necessary to deliver the service (e.g., payment processing, email delivery) and with the California Secretary of State as part of your filing.

Public records note

The contents of a filed Statement of Information become public record with the State of California — that is true no matter how the form is filed, by us or by you.

Retention & your choices

We retain order records and filed copies so we can provide you proof of filing and deadline reminders. You may unsubscribe from reminders at any time via the link in any reminder email, and you may request deletion of your data by emailing support@statementdue.com (we may retain records we are legally required to keep).
