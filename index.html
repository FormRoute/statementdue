Common questions

Can't I just file this myself for free?

Yes — you can always file directly at bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov and pay only the state fee ($20–$25). People use StatementDue because we prepare the form correctly, submit it, keep a copy on record, and remind them before the next deadline — so it never gets forgotten. (As of August 2026, online filing also requires setting up Secretary of State account access, which we help you avoid dealing with.)

When is my Statement of Information due?

Corporations file every year, LLCs every two years, during a 6-month window ending in the anniversary month of your registration. New entities must also file within 90 days of forming. Use our free deadline checker above and we'll tell you your exact window.

What happens if I miss the deadline?

The Franchise Tax Board assesses a $250 penalty after the Secretary of State reports the entity delinquent, and continued non-filing can lead to suspension — losing good standing, the ability to enforce contracts, and ultimately liability protection until reinstated.

How do you submit my filing?

We prepare the official state form with the information you provide and submit it to the California Secretary of State with your state fee, typically within 1 business day of your order. You receive a copy of the completed form and confirmation of submission by email.

Is my information safe?

We collect only what the state form requires — no SSNs, no financial accounts. Statement of Information contents become public record with the state once filed (that's true no matter how you file). Payment is handled by Stripe; we never see your card number.

Are you affiliated with the State of California?

No. StatementDue is a private document-preparation and filing service operated by FormRoute LLC. We are not a government agency, and we are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice.
